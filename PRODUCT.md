# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Hiring managers, recruiters, and technical leads evaluating a frontend engineer. They scan quickly, then dive into selected sections for proof of capability and craft.

## Product Purpose
Present Dean Barquio's work, experience, and capabilities with a memorable portfolio that feels premium while staying easy to navigate. Success means visitors can understand strengths fast and confidently reach out.

## Brand Personality
Precise, confident, modern.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS template layouts with repetitive metric cards
- Heavy decorative effects that reduce readability
- Overcrowded sections with weak hierarchy

## Design Principles
- Clarity first: key information is understandable within seconds.
- Craft in details: spacing, typography, and motion feel deliberate.
- Evidence over claims: projects and experience prove capability.
- Consistent rhythm: sections balance density and breathing room.
- Fast scanning, deep reading: surfaces support both behaviors.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and focus visibility. Preserve keyboard navigation for interactive elements. Keep motion subtle and respect reduced-motion preferences where possible.
